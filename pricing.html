The anatomy of a ski / snowboard

Process

  • Step 1: Prep any base repairs needed. Need a decent 'v' shape in any repair, to get good adhesive bond. Use metal scraper to create 'v' and remove old wax and debris.
  • Step 2: Clean off old wax and debris from the base of the skis or snowboard. Use wax removal spray, heat and scrape.
  • Step 3: 3 methods for repairing the base's damaged scratched and gouged areas:-
    • Method 1: Use a P-Tex strip, lighter and metal scraper. Heat the base repair material, with the lighter, hold over the metal scraper to increase the heat until the flame turns blue and then drip the melted P-Tex into the damaged area. Immediately place the metal scraper over the repair and let it cool. Then scrape off the excess with the metal scraper.
    • Method 2: Use a P-Tex strip and a soldering iron (use 75W-140W heavy duty iron with a 4.8mm chisel tip), scrape the melted P-Tex into the damaged area with the soldering iron. Let it cool and then scrape off the excess. The soldering iron option, in my opinion, gives the best results for accuracy, ease of use and gives the best results.
    • Method 3: Use a base repair gun, with the repair material heated in the gun that can be used to fill in scratches and gouges in the base of the skis or snowboard. This can be a better option for larger repairs as it can be bulky to use for small repairs (and the gun is expensive to buy).
  • Step 4: Once removed the excess with the metal scraper, use sandpaper (Mirka Abranet 180 grit hook and loop base sanding sheets) to smooth the repair to add consistency and structure to the base before waxing. Always sand up and down the length of the ski or snowboard, never across or in circles.
  • Step 5: Edge the base and side edges of the skis or snowboard. Start with the base edges, then side edges.
    • Base edges: Use the base/side edge tool and set to 1° with a file to sharpen the edges and set the base edge to 1°. Ensure the file's arrow is in the correct direction. Run the base edge tool along the length of the ski, no need to apply pressure, just keep running the tool along the length until the tool glides smoothlu along and you hear 'sound' - you'll know.
    • Base edges: Use a diamond stone to remove any burrs and polish the base edges.
    • Base edges: Repeat the process for the other side of the ski.
    • Side edges: Use the base/side edge tool and set to 1°, 2° or 3° (see Anatomy of a Ski/Snowboard section for explanation of side degrees) with a file to sharpen the edges and set the side edge to 1°, 2° or 3°. Ensure the file's arrow is in the correct direction. Run the base/side edge tool along the length of the ski, no need to apply pressure, just keep running the tool along the length until the tool glides smoothly along and you hear 'sound' - you'll know.
    • Side edges: Use a diamond stone to remove any burrs and polish the side edges.
    • Side edges: Repeat the process for the other side of the ski.
    • NB: You can clean the diamond stone with a simple pencil rubber.
  • Step 6: Use a Gummi block to de-tune the tip and tail of the ski or snowboard. Just gently rub up and down the tip and tail from just where the tip and tail staert to turn up.
  • Step 7: Clean the base again with the Wax AB cleaning spray.
  • Step 8: Wax the base of the skis or snowboard. Use a waxing iron (set to 120°C) to melt the wax and drip it onto the base of the ski or snowboard. Have the wax and the iron at 45°. Use the waxing iron to spread the wax evenly over the base. First couple of runs, just get the wax on the base. Then do a couple of passes with the waxing iron, in one long, slow motion along the complete length of the ski. Let the wax cool, for 20 minutes or so, for the heat to absorb the wax and then scrape off the excess with a plastic scraper, from the base and the edges.
  • Step 9: Use a Nylon brush first to remove any remaining wax and to polish the base.
  • Step 10: Use a horse hair brush to polish the base and remove any remaining wax.
  • Step 11: Use the nylon brush one last time to polish the base
